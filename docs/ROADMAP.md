# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Playable vertical slice
Solo challenge, 1v1 AI, ball physics, vertical-ring scoring, mobile controls, match flow.

## Phase 2 — Visual identity
Modular carved-stone arena, sunset lighting, optimized textures, ball trail, impact particles, stronger character silhouettes.

## Phase 3 — Character feel
Real idle/run/jump/strike/hit/KO animation clips, Striker controller, responsive camera, audio feedback.

## Phase 4 — Combat and abilities
Shoulder charge, energy meter, Hip Thrust, Solar Burst, Jaguar Pounce, readable cooldowns and hit feedback.

## Phase 5 — 3v3 demo
Striker/Tank/Support roles, team AI, pass-chain and kinetic-charge systems, team selection, match timer.

## Phase 6 — Polish
Crowd impostors, ambient life, performance tiers, PWA/offline support, accessibility and iPhone Safari QA.
